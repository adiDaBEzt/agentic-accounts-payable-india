Problem Statement

Accounts Payable is still a manual, leaky process

Invoices arrive by email in every layout imaginable, get keyed into the ERP by hand, and are paid without checking what was actually ordered and received.

TODAY What breaks

📥

Unstructured, multi-channel intake

Invoices, POs and receipts land in Gmail as PDFs and scans. Template OCR breaks on tables; AP teams re-key line items.

🔍

No 3-way match before payment

Bills are paid against the PO only — nobody checks the Goods Receipt / Service Entry Sheet. Short shipments and extra service days get paid.

📈

Step-PO ceilings tracked in spreadsheets

Milestone and volume-tier contracts are overbilled because drawdown limits aren't enforced at invoice time.

⏳

Slow ERP posting, zero visibility

3–5 day cycle times forfeit 2/10 Net 30 discounts, and finance can't simply ask about spend, vendors or exceptions.

WITH wxO What we built

🧠

Agentic document processing

watsonx Orchestrate Document Processing extracts header + line items with visual grounding and confidence per field.

✅

Deterministic 2-way & 3-way match

PO ↔ GRN/SES ↔ Invoice checked line-by-line. Variances trigger holds or auto Debit Memos (e.g. DM-2026-7731, −$702).

🔗

Straight-through ERP posting

Clean bills post to SAP / Odoo automatically with GL coding; every record persisted in Neon PostgreSQL.

💬

Conversational AP copilot
